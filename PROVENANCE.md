# Provenance

- **Authorship.** All instructions, reference files, examples and fixtures were written for this project by the author with AI assistance (Claude). No text is copied from papers, textbooks or other skill packages.
- **Examples and fixtures are invented.** Every paper, abstract, Methods paste, policy, dataset and number in `skills/*/examples/` and `evals/` is fictional, written to show or test the skills. None describes a real study.
- **Method names are vocabulary, not citations.** Estimator and test names (Callaway–Sant'Anna, Hansen J, DSL, ERGM, …) refer to well-known methods by their common names; the skills never attach invented quotes, page numbers or DOIs to them.
- **History.** The skills grew out of a private research-portfolio project. An earlier literature-search skill was retired; these two replace it and do not search any database.
